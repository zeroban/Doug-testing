Q: How do I run a report showing volunteer hours by week?
A:
Steps:
1. Navigate to the opportunity page.
2. Click on the "Reports & Imports" tab.
3. Under "Hours Worked by Volunteer," select "Total by Week" from the dropdown.
4. Click the "Download" button to generate the report.

Q: How can I add volunteer hours if there is no live opportunity?
A:
Steps:
1. Go to the "Volunteers" tab on the organization page and search for the volunteer.
2. In their profile, click "Hours Worked."
3. Click "Add Hours Worked."
4. Select the opportunity, enter the hours worked, and submit.

Q: How do I set up shift reminder automations?
A:
There are two levels of shift reminder automations:

- Organization Level:
  1. Navigate to Email Communication > Manage Automation.
  2. Click "Create New Automation" and select "Pre/Post Shift."
  3. Set the recipient(s), timing, and automation name.

- Opportunity Level:
  1. Go to the specific opportunity.
  2. Click Email Communications > Manage Automations.
  3. Click "Create New Automation," select "Pre/Post Shift," and follow the same steps.

Q: How do I create or send an email?
A: 
To create and send an email while on the organization level, follow these steps: 
  1. Navigate to the "Volunteers" tab at the organization level.
  2. Filter or check the box next to the volunteers you would like to send the email to.
  3. Once the desired volunteers are selected, click the "Actions" button.
  4. In the dropdown menu, hover over the "Send" button, and on the right-hand side of it, click "Send Message."
You can then design the email to look and say what you need it to. While in the pop-up window, you can also select a template you have created on the right-hand side.

To create and send an email while on the opportunity level, follow these steps: 
  1. While on the opportunity level, navigate to the "Volunteers" tab.
  2. Filter or check the box next to the volunteers you would like to send the email to.
  3. Once the desired volunteers are selected, click the "Actions" button.
  4. In the dropdown menu, hover over the "Send" button, and on the right-hand side of it, click "Send Message."
You can then design the email to look and say what you need it to. While in the pop-up window, you can also select a template you have created on the right-hand side.
