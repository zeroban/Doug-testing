Q: Why aren't shifts visible to volunteers even though shift visibility is turned on?
A: 
- Check if the Preview Shifts toggle is turned off.
- To enable it:
  1. Navigate to the Opportunity Dashboard.
  2. Click on Page Settings.
  3. Scroll down and ensure the Preview Shifts toggle is turned ON.

Q: Can I create a live opportunity without any shifts and allow volunteers to record their own hours?
A: 
Yes. Volunteers can sign up and manually add their hours once enrolled.
Steps for Volunteers:
1. After signing up, go to their profile icon (top right) and select "My Hours Worked."
2. Click the green "Add Hours Worked" button, select the opportunity, and manually enter hours.
Bonus Tip: Make sure the "Self Track Hours" option is enabled in Attendance Settings under the opportunity’s General Settings.

Q: I can't see the person I want to assign as the new Opportunity Manager. What should I do?
A:
Make sure the person has the Event Manager role.
Steps:
1. Go to Roles & Qualifications on the Opportunity Dashboard.
2. Select Event Manager > Take Action > Edit Volunteers.
3. Assign the person as an Event Manager and save changes. They will now appear in the Primary Manager dropdown in Page Settings.

Q: How do I bulk assign roles to volunteers?
A:
Roles can only be assigned in bulk from the opportunity page.
Steps:
1. Open the opportunity page and click Opportunity List.
2. Select volunteers to assign roles to.
3. Click the Actions button > Bulk Assign > Roles.

Q: How can a volunteer change their email address without creating a new profile?
A:
Volunteers can update their email address themselves.
Steps:
1. Log in and click the profile icon.
2. Select "Edit Profile" > scroll to Profile Contact > click "Edit Username" next to the email address.
